# Battlecard

What we want said about us, in the words to say it. Every off-page ask gets checked
against this file. Without it, outreach produces inconsistency, and inconsistency is
the thing answer engines punish most directly.

## Ideal positioning

Plain words an engine would actually use. Not internal vocabulary, not aspirational
copy. If this says "the operating system for revenue teams", every mention you
influence will say that, and no buyer searches for it.
