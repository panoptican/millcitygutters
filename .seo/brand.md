## Product

- **Name:** Mill City Gutters
- **One-liner (≤20 words):** Seamless gutter installation, protection, and custom fabrication for Minneapolis and Twin Cities homes and businesses.
- **What we do:** Fabricate and install seamless gutters on site — 5-inch and 6-inch aluminum, copper, K-style, half-round and oversized — plus gutter-protection systems and tear-off of old gutters. Full-service: measure, fabricate, install, clean up.
- **Pricing structure:** Simple per-foot rate on installed gutter and downspout, plus a separate per-foot tear-off rate. No line-item fees for miters, drop-downs, screws, hangers, or labor. Exact rates are not published on the site. (`index.html` "Transparent Costs")
- **Free tier?** N/A — free on-site quote (inferred; site says "send us your project details and we will get a quote to you").

## Audience

- **Primary persona:** Twin Cities homeowners (owner-occupiers, 30-70) replacing failing gutters or adding protection before winter.
- **Secondary personas:** Small commercial / multi-unit property owners and managers; homeowners doing an exterior remodel or a copper/half-round aesthetic upgrade.
- **Industries we target:** Residential and light commercial property in Minneapolis, Saint Paul, and the Twin Cities metro.
- **Company size we target:** N/A.
- **Jobs to be done (top 3):**
  1. Replace rusted, leaking, sagging, or undersized gutters with seamless ones before they damage the house.
  2. Stop climbing a ladder twice a year — install gutter protection so leaves and ice stop clogging the system.
  3. Get a straight, honest per-foot price and a clean install from someone who has done every roof type.

## Anti-positioning (where we don't compete)

1. We are not a full exterior remodeler — no roofing, siding, or windows.
2. We are not the cheapest quote; we compete on a straight price and a clean install.
3. We are not a supply-only or DIY gutter-parts shop.
4. We are not a door-to-door national franchise with a hard close.
5. We are not emergency-only; we do planned replacement and protection.
6. We are not a commercial-only contractor — homes are the core of the work.

## Concrete differentiators

1. On-site fabrication to the exact dimensions of the building — truly seamless runs.
2. One simple per-foot rate; no nickel-and-diming on miters, hangers, screws, or labor.
3. Experience across every Twin Cities roof type (hip, gable, flat, gambrel, mansard).
4. Local Minneapolis shop (5237 34th Ave S) — not a national call center.
